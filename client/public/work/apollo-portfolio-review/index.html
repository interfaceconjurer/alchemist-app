02Salesforce Development Plugin

The real problem

Two gates kept non-experts out.

Gate 1
A frictionless way in.

Flow straight into building on Salesforce, headlessly.

Gate 2
Knowing what’s possible.

Clear wayfinding, and a smart next step.

Out of that came my interaction model: Fixed / Guided / Free, each chosen by trading off the risk of being wrong against the risk of being noisy.

Fixed

Must be recognizable at a glance or have zero chance of hallucination. Org and project are always shown the same way; deterministic paints identical every time.

Guided

Low risk but complex. The agent gets context, and the user gets a predictable layout.

Free

Low risk and light. The agent picks up intent without signposting, because false positives get noisy fast.

risk of being wrongrisk of being noisy

BuiltFixedGuided

The Fixed / Guided / Free model. Claude Code lifecycle hooks render deterministic Python paints, so your org, your project, and what’s possible always show up the same way.

Follow-through: I retired my own six-stage journey rail. Development isn’t linear, and two stages could never be truthfully reached.

It became confidence-scored journey hints: one nudge at a time, low-confidence ones never shown.

See what happened: every change, every step, on the record.
